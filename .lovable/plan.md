

# Sitzu Assure — Insurance Made Simple

## Vision
A warm, trust-building insurance website that strips away jargon and makes protection feel like a natural, empowering choice — not a chore.

## Brand & Design
- **Palette**: Warm, approachable tones — soft teal/green primary (trust & calm), warm white backgrounds, gentle gradients. Avoid cold corporate blues.
- **Typography**: Clean, rounded sans-serif headings (friendly authority), relaxed body text.
- **Tone**: Conversational, reassuring, human. "We've got your back" energy. Zero jargon.
- **Imagery vibe**: Abstract shields, gentle illustrations, warm icons — not stock photos of suits shaking hands.

## Pages & Features

### 1. Landing / Home Page
- **Hero section**: Bold headline like *"Protection shouldn't be complicated."* with a clear CTA split: "I'm a Business Owner" / "I want to protect my gadgets"
- **Trust bar**: Social proof numbers (businesses helped, claims processed, etc.)
- **How It Works**: 3-step visual flow — simple, scannable
- **Testimonials**: Real-feeling quotes from SMB owners and gadget users
- **FAQ section**: Addressing common fears in plain language
- **Footer**: Links, contact, trust badges

### 2. Policy Document Helper (for SMBs)
- **Headline**: *"Finally understand what your policy actually says."*
- **Upload/paste area**: Users can paste policy text or describe their policy
- **Plain-language breakdown**: Simulated output showing how complex policy language gets translated into simple summaries (UI mockup — AI integration can come later)
- **Key highlights**: What's covered, what's not, important dates, action items
- **CTA**: "Get expert help" or "Talk to an advisor"

### 3. Gadget Insurance Guide (for end users)
- **Headline**: *"Your phone is your life. Shouldn't it be protected?"*
- **Interactive gadget selector**: Pick your device type (phone, laptop, tablet, smartwatch)
- **Risk calculator/quiz**: Fun, quick questions that reveal how much a user stands to lose without coverage — makes it personal
- **Coverage explainer cards**: What protection looks like, in plain English
- **Pricing teaser / CTA**: "See plans" or "Get a quote"

### 4. Shared Components
- **Navbar**: Logo, nav links (Home, For Businesses, For Gadgets, About), CTA button
- **Conversion-focused design**: Sticky CTA, social proof sprinkled throughout, urgency without pressure
- **Mobile-first responsive layout**

## What This Delivers
A polished, conversion-optimized MVP that makes two audiences feel seen: the overwhelmed business owner drowning in policy docs, and the everyday person who hasn't thought about protecting their devices yet. No jargon, no fear — just clarity and confidence.
